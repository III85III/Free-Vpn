III85III VPN

Open the app and add your own Mozilla account.
Use your Mozilla password, not a Google password.
If a code is requested, enter it in the separate verification window.
Choose a location and connection mode, then connect.

App rules in this engine are exclusions: checked apps bypass VPN.
No personal VPS is required. Mozilla eligibility, regions and quotas apply.

The interface, installer, and core engine are published by III85III.
Read licenses/GPL-3.0.txt and the included source files.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+1 through Ctrl+6 switch pages; Ctrl+F searches locations
or app rules; Escape clears the current filter; F1 opens Help.

Mozilla authentication and service availability depend on your account
and network. This release does not guarantee that every sign-in succeeds.
